About Anyon inc.

Independent. Professional. Dependable.

Based in Quebec, Anyon inc. is an independent corporation dedicated to helping businesses streamline their operations. We specialize in providing administrative, logistical, and professional support to companies that need an extra pair of capable hands.

Whether you need ongoing operational assistance or short-term project support, we operate on a foundation of clear communication, reliability, and strict confidentiality. We don't just complete tasks—we integrate with your workflow to make things easier.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with us.

Do you only work with businesses in Quebec?

While we are incorporated and based in Quebec, we offer remote services and can support businesses across Canada and internationally.

How do we get started?

Simply reach out via our contact form or email. We will schedule a brief introductory call to discuss your needs and see how we can help.

Are you a staffing agency?

No. Anyon inc. is an independent service corporation. We take on specific projects and provide professional services under a B2B contract, rather than supplying temporary employees.

How is billing handled?

We invoice on a project-by-project basis or through a monthly retainer, depending on our agreement. All invoices are billed to your business entity.
