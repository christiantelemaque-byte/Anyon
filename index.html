About Anyon inc.

Independent. Professional. Dependable.

Based in Quebec, Anyon inc. is an independent corporation dedicated to helping businesses and individuals streamline their finances and operations. Backed by over 14 years of personal tax experience and equipped with professional corporate tax software (WebTax4B), we provide reliable administrative, logistical, and accounting support.

Whether you need ongoing operational assistance, short-term project support, or expert tax preparation for personal, independent, or corporate filings, we operate on a foundation of clear communication, reliability, and strict confidentiality. We don't just complete tasks—we ensure your financials are in order so you can focus on growth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about working with us.

Do you handle both personal and corporate taxes?

Yes. We prepare personal tax returns (T1/TP1), independent contractor returns, and corporate returns (T2/CO-17). We use professional, government-certified software to ensure accuracy and electronic filing.

Do you only work with businesses in Quebec?

While we are incorporated and based in Quebec, we offer remote services and can support businesses and individuals across Canada.

How do we get started?

Simply reach out via our contact form, email, or WhatsApp. We will schedule a brief introductory call to discuss your needs and see how we can help.

Are you a staffing agency?

No. Anyon inc. is an independent service corporation. We take on specific projects and provide professional services under a B2B contract, rather than supplying temporary employees.
